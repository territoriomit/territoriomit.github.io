a conversation on the qualitative housing deficit and the layers of informality in argentina

Our first event brought together a conversation on the qualitative housing deficit and the layers of informality in Argentina. The panel examined how we conceptualize this deficit and identify which policy instruments actually move the needle, considering both financing and implementation.

Gathering at the DUSP City Arena, we were proud to host this dialogue with the MIT Department of Urban Studies and Planning, the Harvard Argentine Student Society (HASS), and LatinGSD. This event marks the first step for Territorio in fostering discussion on urban challenges across the region.
