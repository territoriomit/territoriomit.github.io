Ways to get involved

However you come to Latin American cities, whether through research, practice, policy, design, or simple curiosity, there's a place for you here.

Join the community. Territorio is open to all MIT students, researchers, and affiliates. Write to us and we'll add you to our list so you hear about events first.

Propose a speaker or a topic. We want the conversation to reflect the full diversity of the region, from the Andean highlands to the Amazon basin, from the Southern Cone megacities to the mid-sized cities that rarely make it into planning curricula.

Co-host with us. We build bridges across departments at MIT and with partners at Harvard and beyond. If your group works on housing, climate, transportation, or spatial justice, let's talk.

Write for the blog. We publish opinion, articles, and publications from the community.
